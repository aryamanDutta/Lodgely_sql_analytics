Complete Hotel Operations Architecture

Built from the ground up on MySQL 8.0 in Third Normal Form (3NF) with ACID transactional integrity, server-side date validation, and responsive Jinja2 interfaces.

Reservation Lifecycle

Create, update, check-in, check-out, and cancel bookings with pessimistic row-locking (SELECT ... FOR UPDATE) preventing race conditions.

Room Inventory & Availability

Full inventory catalog across Single, Double, Deluxe, and Suite rooms. Filter by date-range to query real availability via SQL subqueries.
